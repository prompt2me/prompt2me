Use this file when another skill needs audience context without the full Audience Profile — either a compact card for quick reference, or a structured handoff when passing findings to another marketing skill.

## Handoff rules

Send relevant outputs to:

- `managing-business-context` for durable business or market changes.
- `defining-brand-system` for audience language, needs, positioning implications, and messaging constraints.
- `researching-markets` for unanswered market questions.
- `analyzing-competitors` for alternatives and competitive expectations.
- `developing-marketing-strategy` for segment priorities and strategic implications.
- `positioning-offers` for differentiation and value proposition work.
- `developing-content-strategy` for content themes and audience needs.
- `writing-marketing-copy` for customer language, objections, motivations, and message angles.
- `briefing-campaigns` for campaign audiences, jobs, triggers, and desired actions.
- `planning-channel-mix` for channel hypotheses and audience context.
- `designing-marketing-experiments` for assumptions that require testing.
